# Final research verdict

1. **Implemented:** baseline IV/SC, minimal and whole-body PBPK, transport, FcRn,
   effective soluble/membrane TMDD, regional hypotheses, optional ADA clearance,
   reduced fitting/inference, an interactive 15-page platform, reporting and figures.
2. **Scientifically supported mechanisms:** established antibody transport, lymph,
   salvage and target-binding concepts; minimal-model structure checked against
   a primary source. Human whole-body numerical parameters remain largely assumed.
3. **Actually obtained:** immutable OSP snapshot/reports; 174 aggregate mouse records;
   two human articles supplying five NCA summary facts; TAURUS article/metadata;
   tissue-study metadata/abstracts; EMA Humira information. No private patient data.
4. **Independent experimental observations:** two correlated human calibration
   summaries and three correlated holdout summaries from **one held-out trial**.
   No raw individual human concentration records. Five terminal mouse points are
   nested within the eight-point fitting profile; 174 retained records are not
   174 independent validation cases. Usable independent human tissue observations: **0**.
5. **Plasma accuracy:** the reduced healthy-summary comparison underpredicts held-out
   AUC_inf 20.1%, Cmax 15.5%, and AUC_0_336 12.4%. Full profiles, IBD populations and
   clinical prediction-interval coverage are not externally validated.
6. **Intestinal accuracy:** unknown; no usable independent tissue validation.
7. **Disease-model superiority:** not demonstrated. Synthetic free-colon results
   depend on the generating transport assumption; plasma gains are not uniform.
8. **Infliximab:** not validated. The UI offers only an acknowledged generic IV scaffold.
9. **Unidentifiable mechanisms:** absolute SC F/CL/V scaling; weak whole-body
   mechanism combinations at the stated practical threshold; unsupported target,
   physiology and population parameters cannot be estimated precisely here.
10. **Novelty:** unproven. Established mechanisms are not new; full prior-art
    differentiation and independent transfer evidence are incomplete.
11. **Publication readiness:** not ready as a validated IBD tissue or clinical study.
    A technical/software submission needs completed positioning, reference
    reproduction, independent review and author licensing/release decisions.
12. **Highest-value next experiments:** independent matched biopsy/serum datasets
    with full dosing/time/segment/activity/assay recovery; external vascular/lymph
    physiology and functional FcRn/TNF kinetics; unseen cohorts; molecule-specific
    infliximab records; donor-aware expression linked to protein/function evidence.
